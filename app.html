FeeDrag

A fee of 1% sounds small. See what it takes from your balance over the years, next to cheaper options.
Each year: balance = (balance + added) x (1 + return) x (1 - fee). The SEC Investor Bulletin example ($100,000, 4% a year, 20 years) gives about $208,000, $198,000 and $179,000 for 0.25%, 0.50% and 1.00%. Returns are a constant assumption, not a forecast, and real fees can include trading costs and taxes that are not modeled. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
