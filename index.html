FeeDrag
One percent a year sounds like nothing. Over 20 years it is a third of your gains.
Open the app
$100,000 at 4% for 20 years grows to $179,213 with a 1% fee and $208,413 with a 0.25% fee. The 1% fee costs $39,899 against no fee.

What it does

  • Compares three fee levels on the same balance, yearly additions and return
  • Shows dollars lost, share of the final balance and share of your gains
  • Draws the balance curves so the gap is visible
  • Matches the SEC Investor Bulletin example
Method checked against the SEC Investor Bulletin "How Fees and Expenses Affect Your Investment Portfolio" (investor.gov). Constant-return assumption, not a forecast or advice. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
